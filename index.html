// Field Glossary
TERMINOLOGY
UAP
Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena — the US government's modern term, replacing UFO. Covers aerial, maritime, and transmedium objects.
AARO
All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office. DOD's current UAP investigation unit, founded 2022. Caseload now exceeds 2,000.
NHI
Non-Human Intelligence — term used by whistleblowers and some officials to describe alleged non-terrestrial entities or their technology.
IMMACULATE CONSTELLATION
Alleged classified government program said to hold thousands of high-resolution UAP images and videos, per journalist Michael Shellenberger's 2026 congressional testimony.
TRANSMEDIUM
Objects observed operating across multiple domains — air, sea, and space — without apparent propulsion or transition signatures.
NUFORC
National UFO Reporting Center — civilian organisation tracking and archiving public UAP sightings since 1974.
DISCLOSURE
The process by which governments declassify and publish records relating to UAP encounters, recovered materials, or related research programs.
WHISTLEBLOWER
Former government or military personnel who claim inside knowledge of concealed UAP programs, such as David Grusch or Luis Elizondo.
